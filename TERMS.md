# Task Companion 使用条款 / Terms of Use

更新日期 / Updated: 2026-11-09

## 中文

Task Companion 是按现状提供的开源任务管理工具。你需要自行核对任务、进度与 ChatGPT 的建议。请只输入你有权使用的内容，并遵守相关平台的使用条款。你可以随时停止使用并在网页中删除自己的任务数据。软件源码按 [MIT 许可证](LICENSE) 提供。

Task Companion is an open-source task organizer provided as-is. You are responsible for checking your tasks, progress, and ChatGPT's suggestions. Enter only content you are entitled to use and follow the applicable platform terms. You may stop using the service and delete your task data in the web app at any time.
