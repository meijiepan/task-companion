# Support / 支持

如遇到任务丢失、无法登录或插件不能打开，请保留页面地址、操作步骤与错误文字，在 [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/meijiepan/task-companion/issues) 提交。不要提交私人任务内容、运行密钥或聊天全文。

For help, open an [Issue](https://github.com/meijiepan/task-companion/issues). Include steps to reproduce and the visible error; do not include private task text or credentials.
